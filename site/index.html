The model

Two numbers, one finished thought.

A typing test measures transcription: the sentence already exists. Fastfingaz has no target text, so it measures the composition — and keeps the pause in the reading.

Procedure

Install it, write something real, send it.

There is no test paragraph and no target sentence. The first honest baseline is your own next prompt.

01 / INSTALL

Load the unpacked extension.

Download the latest release, unzip it, and load the folder from chrome://extensions. No account, no server.

02 / TYPE

Write in the ChatGPT composer.

The HUD docks in the empty gutter beside the conversation. It shows the live reading, the cadence meter and your baseline.

03 / SEND

Print the receipt.

The sent prompt becomes a paper receipt: think, flow, peak, words, time and signal. Copy or download it in three social formats.

Artifact

The receipt is the share card.

A completed prompt is printed, not graded. The card carries the serial, the barcode, the cadence bars and the baseline — and it is drawn on your machine.

Questions

The short answers.

What the two clocks mean, and what happens to your words.

Is Fastfingaz a typing test?

No. A typing test gives you the words and starts a timer. Fastfingaz gives you a blank ChatGPT composer, so it measures how quickly you turn your own thought into text. Flow and peak are the numbers to compare with a typing test; think WPM is deliberately slower because it includes the thinking.

What is the difference between think WPM and flow WPM?

Think WPM is the finished message divided by total wall time, including every pause, re-read and rewrite. Flow WPM is inserted characters divided by active typing time, with long gaps clamped. The gap between them is the cost of choosing the words.

Does Fastfingaz store my prompt text?

Not by default. It stores WPM, word counts, timings and confidence locally. The optional Store text setting keeps the sent prompt on your machine only; nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work outside ChatGPT?

Not yet. The 0.3.x builds attach to chatgpt.com. A per-tab mode for other text boxes is on the roadmap; it needs a separate permission and a generic end-of-run adapter.

Is it free?

Yes. The early build is free and MIT licensed. There is no account, subscription or server.
