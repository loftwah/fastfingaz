The model

Two numbers, one finished thought.

A typing test measures transcription: the sentence already exists. Thinktype has no target text, so it measures the composition — and keeps the pause in the reading.

THE TWO CLOCKS SAMPLE READING

THINK WPM

The whole message, from the first keystroke to send. Every re-read and every “what am I actually trying to say” is included.

FLOW WPM

Your hands-only cadence. Long gaps are clamped, not erased: a pause still costs something without swallowing the session.

Procedure

Install it, write something real, send it.

There is no test paragraph and no target sentence. The first honest baseline is your own next prompt.

01 / INSTALL

Load the unpacked extension.

Download the latest release, unzip it, and load the folder from chrome://extensions. No account, no server.

02 / TYPE

Write in the ChatGPT composer.

The HUD docks in the empty gutter beside the conversation. It shows the live reading, the cadence meter and your baseline.

03 / SEND

Print the receipt.

The sent prompt becomes a paper receipt: think, flow, peak, words, time and signal. Copy or download it in three social formats.

Artifact

The receipt is the share card.

A completed prompt is printed, not graded. The card carries the serial, the barcode, the cadence bars and the baseline — and it is drawn on your machine.

Privacy

Local by construction.

The extension has no server to send anything to. It asks Chrome for two permissions and only touches the ChatGPT composer.

WHAT LEAVES THIS MACHINE

  • NO SERVERNo account, no sync, no backend to secure.
  • NO ANALYTICSNo telemetry, no remote logging, no third-party scripts.
  • TEXT OPT-INPrompt text is not stored unless you explicitly enable it.
  • YOUR EXPORTMetrics live in Chrome's local extension storage; you can export or clear them.
