## Store Listing

**Extension Name** [REQUIRED]

Thinktype — real typing speed

**Short Description** [REQUIRED]

Measure how fast you turn a thought into text in ChatGPT. Think WPM, flow WPM, printed receipts. Local-only.

**Detailed Description** [REQUIRED]

Thinktype measures how fast you compose your own words in ChatGPT — not how fast you can copy a sentence from a typing test.

Every prompt gives you two readings. Think WPM is the whole message, thinking pauses included. Flow WPM is your hands-only cadence: the speed you type when the words are already there. The gap between them is the interesting part.

A pause is not a failed run. The live reading is the last eight seconds of actual typing, so it holds while you think instead of bleeding down. The idle big number is labelled LAST THINK, because it is the finished-thought score, not your typing speed. A slower run never overwrites a personal best.

Key features:
• A tall, bottom-anchored instrument that lives in the empty gutter beside the ChatGPT conversation, with a 38-bar cadence meter and a 72–120 px live WPM reading
• A draggable WPM orb for the collapsed state: click to expand, drag to move, resize from 44–104 px with the corner grip; position and size persist
• Live WPM measured over the last eight seconds of actual typing: short prompts are not diluted, think gaps hold the number, and manual pauses are excluded
• A manual pause button and Alt+Shift+P shortcut: the clocks freeze, the number holds, and the break is excluded from the final reading
• Think WPM, flow WPM, peak, best, weighted 20-session average, words, time, signal and consistency
• A printed receipt after every sent prompt, with a serial, barcode and a small NEW BEST stamp
• Share cards in three designed formats — 1200×630, 1080×1080 and 1080×1920 — with a share sheet, live preview, copy and PNG download
• Durable local history: best think/flow/peak, last eligible run, rolling average and all-time average
• Controls for your thinking threshold, minimum message length, HUD position and privacy

How to use it:
1. Install Thinktype and open chatgpt.com.
2. Start a new chat and type a real prompt. The HUD sits in the bottom gutter and measures from the first keystroke.
3. Send the message. Thinktype prints a receipt.
4. Open SHARE CARD to choose a format, or open the popup for your history.

Privacy:
Thinktype has no server, no account and no analytics. Your prompt text is not stored by default; the optional Store text setting keeps it on this machine only. Your typing metrics live in Chrome's local extension storage and never leave the browser unless you export them yourself.

If you find a bug or have an idea, open an issue at https://github.com/loftwah/thinktype/issues.

## Permissions Justification

| Permission | Type | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `storage` | permissions | Saves your settings and local session history (WPM, word counts, timings, the durable best/average summary) in Chrome's extension storage. An in-progress snapshot uses `chrome.storage.session` and contains no prompt text. Nothing is sent off-device. |
| `clipboardWrite` | permissions | Copies the locally rendered share card (landscape, square or story) to your clipboard when you click Copy image. It is only used on that click. |
| `https://chatgpt.com/*` | host_permissions | Injects the floating HUD and reads the ChatGPT composer to measure the prompt you are writing. It does not read other tabs or other sites. |
| `https://chat.openai.com/*` | host_permissions | Legacy ChatGPT domain. The site redirects to chatgpt.com, but the HUD must still attach for users on the old domain. |

No permissions were added in v0.2.0 or v0.2.1.

## Review Notes

### Known Issues / Limitations

- IME input (Chinese, Japanese, etc.) is flagged as approximate and lowers the signal score. An IME run is excluded from best below 85% signal.
- Editing an existing draft is measured from the next keystroke; the earlier text is not counted in the WPM. Resumed drafts are kept in a draft bucket and excluded from best and averages.
- ChatGPT UI changes can break composer detection until the selector fallbacks are updated.
- Share cards are generated locally at 1200×630, 1080×1080 and 1080×1920; no image service is used.
- The HUD docks in the empty gutter beside the conversation. At narrow widths it uses a slim strip; if the sidebar is open and there is no gutter, it may use the sidebar space. It can be dragged, and Reset HUD position restores the dock.
- The 1280×800 store screenshot is a sample composition; replace it with real captures before submission.
- The Chrome Web Store listing has not been submitted for review.
