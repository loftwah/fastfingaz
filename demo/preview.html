thinktype

Your real typing speed,
while you're actually thinking.

A Chrome extension that measures how fast you compose your own words in ChatGPT. No test paragraphs. No memorising. Think WPM versus flow WPM.

Share card · generated locally
Think WPM

Finished words per minute across the whole message, thinking pauses included.

Flow WPM

Your hands-only cadence: the speed you type when the words are already there.

Local only

No server, no account. Metrics stay in the browser. Text is not stored by default.
