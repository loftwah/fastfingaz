Can you explain why the composer is the right place to measure composition?
Because the composer is where the thought stops being private and becomes text. A typing test hands you the sentence; a conversation makes you find it. The interesting number is not how fast your hands can move — it is how much of the finished thought survives the pause.
So the gap between think and flow is the product?
The gap is the reading. Think WPM includes every re-read and every “what am I actually trying to say”. Flow WPM is your hands when the words are already there. One is the finished thought; the other is the instrument. Keep both.
