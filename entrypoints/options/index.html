SETTINGS / REV. 0.1.0 LOCAL ONLY / NO SERVER

MEASUREMENT

THE SIGNAL MODEL

2.0s

Gaps longer than this count as thinking and are excluded from flow WPM. Lower it for stricter numbers; raise it if you pause mid-sentence.

Quick “thanks” messages are conversation, not a reading.

CHARS

HUD

THE FLOATING READOUT

The HUD docks to the bottom of the viewport and lives in the empty gutter beside the conversation. It shrinks to a slim strip when the gutter is narrow, and never covers the composer or your last message. Drag the full HUD by its header, or drag the collapsed orb by its face. Drag the orb’s bottom-right grip to resize it (44–104px), or focus the grip and use the arrow keys. Pause or resume the measurement with Alt+Shift+P; the pause button is in the HUD header.

PRIVACY

WHAT LEAVES THIS MACHINE

Thinktype has no server. Numbers and text stay in this browser unless you export them yourself.
