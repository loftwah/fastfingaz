Measurement

Thinktype measures the gap between how fast your hands move and how fast finished thoughts arrive. These knobs decide where one ends and the other begins.

2.0s

Gaps longer than this are treated as thinking and excluded from flow WPM. Lower it for more punishing numbers; raise it if you pause to think mid-sentence.
